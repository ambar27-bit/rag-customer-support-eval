# NimbusCart - Support & Policies

## Contacting support
Our support team is available by live chat 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. Phone
support is available from 8 AM to 8 PM Eastern Time, Monday through Saturday.
Email support typically responds within 24 hours. The support phone number is
listed at the bottom of every page under "Contact Us".

## Privacy and data
We collect only the information needed to process your orders and improve your
experience. We never sell your personal data to third parties. You can request a
copy of your data or ask us to delete it by contacting privacy support. Payment
card details are encrypted and never stored on our servers in full.

## Fraud and security
If you notice an order you did not place, contact support immediately and we will
freeze the account. We use two-factor authentication, which you can enable in
Account Settings for added security. We will never ask for your full password or
card number by email.

## Promo codes and coupons
Only one promo code can be applied per order. Promo codes cannot be combined with
clearance pricing unless stated otherwise. If a code does not work, check the
expiration date and the minimum order value. Promo codes have no cash value and
cannot be applied after an order is placed.

## Subscriptions
NimbusCart Plus is a subscription offering free express shipping and early access
to sales for 49 dollars per year. You can cancel anytime; cancellation stops the
next renewal but does not refund the current period. Subscription benefits apply
automatically at checkout while active.

## Complaints and escalations
If you are not satisfied with a support interaction, you can ask to escalate to a
supervisor. Formal complaints can be submitted in writing and receive a response
within 3 business days. We aim to resolve all complaints fairly and promptly.
