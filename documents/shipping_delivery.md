# NimbusCart - Shipping & Delivery

## Shipping options and costs
NimbusCart offers three shipping speeds. Standard shipping takes 5 to 7
business days and costs 4.99 dollars, and is free on orders over 50 dollars.
Express shipping takes 2 to 3 business days and costs 12.99 dollars. Overnight
shipping is delivered the next business day if the order is placed before 2 PM
local time, and costs 24.99 dollars.

## Where we ship
We ship to all 50 US states, including Alaska and Hawaii. Orders to Alaska and
Hawaii may take 2 to 4 additional business days. We also ship to Canada and the
United Kingdom; international orders take 10 to 15 business days and incur
customs fees that are the responsibility of the customer.

## Order processing time
Orders are processed within 1 business day. Orders placed on weekends or public
holidays are processed on the next business day. During major sale events such
as Black Friday, processing may take up to 3 business days.

## Tracking your order
Once your order ships, you will receive a tracking number by email. You can also
find tracking details under "My Orders" in your account. Tracking information
may take up to 24 hours to become active after you receive the email.

## Lost or delayed packages
If your package has not arrived within 7 business days of the expected delivery
date, contact support. For packages marked as delivered but not received, we ask
that you wait 48 hours, as carriers sometimes mark packages early. We will open
an investigation with the carrier and issue a replacement or refund if the
package cannot be located.

## Address changes
You can change your shipping address within 1 hour of placing an order by going
to "My Orders" and selecting "Edit Address". After 1 hour, the order enters
fulfillment and the address can no longer be changed.
