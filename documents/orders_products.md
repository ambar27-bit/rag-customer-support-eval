# NimbusCart - Orders & Products

## Cancelling an order
You can cancel an order within 1 hour of placing it, directly from "My Orders"
by clicking "Cancel Order". After 1 hour, the order may already be in
fulfillment and cannot be cancelled; in that case you can refuse delivery or
return the item once it arrives.

## Order confirmation
After placing an order you will receive a confirmation email within 15 minutes.
If you do not receive it, check your spam folder and verify the email on your
account is correct. A missing confirmation email does not mean the order failed.

## Out of stock and backorders
If an item goes out of stock after you order, we will notify you by email. You
can choose to wait for a backorder, substitute a similar item, or cancel for a
full refund. Backordered items ship as soon as stock arrives, usually within 2
to 3 weeks.

## Product warranties
Most electronics come with a 1-year manufacturer warranty covering defects in
materials and workmanship. The warranty does not cover accidental damage, water
damage, or normal wear. Extended warranties can be purchased at checkout for an
additional fee.

## Price adjustments
If an item you bought goes on sale within 7 days of your purchase, we will refund
the difference. Request a price adjustment through support with your order
number. Price adjustments do not apply to clearance or flash-sale items.

## Gift orders and gift wrapping
You can mark any order as a gift at checkout and add a personalized message. Gift
wrapping is available for 3.99 dollars per item. Gift orders include a packing
slip without prices so the recipient does not see the amount paid.

## Bulk and business orders
For orders of 20 or more of the same item, contact our business sales team for
volume pricing. Business accounts can request tax-exempt status by submitting a
valid resale certificate.
