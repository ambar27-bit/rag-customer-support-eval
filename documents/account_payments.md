# NimbusCart - Account & Payments

## Creating an account
You can shop as a guest, but creating an account lets you track orders, save
addresses, and earn rewards points. To create an account, click "Sign Up" and
provide your email and a password of at least 8 characters.

## Accepted payment methods
We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and Discover credit and debit
cards. We also accept PayPal, Apple Pay, and Google Pay. We do not accept checks,
money orders, or cryptocurrency.

## When you are charged
Your card is authorized when you place the order but only charged when the order
ships. For pre-order items, you are charged at the time the order is placed.

## Rewards program
NimbusCart Rewards gives you 1 point for every dollar spent. 100 points equals 5
dollars off a future order. Points expire 12 months after they are earned.
Rewards points cannot be earned on gift card purchases or shipping charges.

## Password reset
If you forgot your password, click "Forgot Password" on the login page and enter
your email. You will receive a reset link that is valid for 30 minutes. If the
link expires, you can request a new one.

## Updating account information
You can update your email, password, and saved addresses under "Account
Settings". Changing your email requires confirming the new address via a
verification link. For security, password changes log you out of all devices.

## Closing your account
To close your account, contact support. Account closure is permanent and removes
your order history and unused rewards points. Any pending orders must be
delivered or cancelled before an account can be closed.
